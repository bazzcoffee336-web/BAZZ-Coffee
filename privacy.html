Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version

  • 🔒 We collect as little as possible: only what we need to send you your ebook and answer you.
  • 🚫 No ads, no tracking cookies, no selling your data. Ever.
  • ✉️ Want your data deleted? Email info@bazzbooks.store and we'll do it.

1. Who we are

BAZZ (bazzbooks.store) sells digital ebooks. For any privacy question, contact info@bazzbooks.store.

2. What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join our list or buy a book, so we can send you the buy link, your download and your receipt.
  • Order details when you buy: the book, the price, the date, and your agreement to the instant download.
  • Messages you send us by email or Instagram, so we can reply.
  • Basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type. Our hosting provider records this automatically to keep the site fast and safe.

We never see or store your card details. Payments are handled by our payment provider.

3. Why we use it

  • to deliver the ebook you bought and send your receipt,
  • to tell you when checkout opens, if you asked us to,
  • to answer your questions and help with problems,
  • to keep the website secure and working, and
  • to meet legal and tax requirements.

4. Who we share it with

We only share data with the services that run our shop, and only what each one needs:

  • Cloudflare: website hosting, security and email forwarding.
  • FormSubmit: delivers the sign-up form to our inbox.
  • Wayl: processes payments.
  • Google: our email inbox, and Search Console, which shows us how people find our site.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

5. Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores two small settings on your own device: that you've already seen the welcome screen, and a short timer that stops the sign-up form being sent twice. They never leave your device.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as the law requires for tax and accounting. Your email for our list is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, delete or download your data, or to stop emailing you, at any time. Email info@bazzbooks.store. If you live in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Our site uses HTTPS encryption and strict security settings. Ebook files are stored privately and are only available to the people who bought them.

9. Children

Our books are written for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 16.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
