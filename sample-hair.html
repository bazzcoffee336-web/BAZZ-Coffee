Chapter 1: "My hair keeps breaking."

The problem

Short broken pieces on your shoulders. Ends that split and snap. Hair that never seems to get longer, even though it's growing.

Why it happens

Hair is strongest when it's dry and weakest when it's wet. Rubbing it with a towel, brushing it while soaked, and pulling knots out fast all snap the strands. Shampoo also removes oils that protect the hair, and without conditioner the strands rub against each other and break.

The fix: handle wet hair like silk

  1. Condition every time you shampoo. Conditioner coats the strands and reduces breakage and split ends.
  2. Pat, don't rub. Wrap your hair in a towel to soak up water, or let it air-dry. Rough rubbing roughs up the hair.
  3. Detangle gently. Use a wide-tooth comb, start at the ends and work up. Never yank through a knot.
  4. Brush less. Brush only to style. The old "100 strokes a day" advice causes breakage.
  5. Trim damaged ends regularly. A split end can't be repaired, only cut, and leaving it can let the split travel further up the strand.

Try it now

Next time you wash your hair, condition it, wrap it in a towel for 10 minutes instead of rubbing, then comb from the ends up with a wide-tooth comb.

Check yourself

  • ☐ I use conditioner every time I shampoo.
  • ☐ I pat or wrap my hair instead of rubbing it.
  • ☐ I detangle from the ends up with a wide-tooth comb.
