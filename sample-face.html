The problem

Every video recommends a different 10-step routine. You buy products, your skin reacts, and you're back where you started.

Why it happens

The beauty industry sells new products every month. But skin needs only a few things every day: to be cleaned gently, to keep its moisture, and to be protected from the sun. Extra steps add more chances of irritation, not more protection.

The fix: the 3-step routine

Morning:

  1. Gentle cleanser (or just lukewarm water if your skin is dry).

  2. Moisturizer.

  3. Sunscreen: broad-spectrum, SPF 30 or higher.

Evening:

  1. Gentle cleanser to remove sunscreen, makeup and sweat.

  2. Moisturizer.

  3. One treatment, only if you need it (for acne, dark spots or lines, see Chapters 3, 6 and 8).

Use lukewarm water, not hot. Pat dry, don't rub.

Try it now

Put every product you own into two groups: the three basics, and "everything else". Use only the basics for the next 7 days.

Check yourself

  • ☐ I have a gentle cleanser, a moisturizer and a sunscreen.
  • ☐ I used only the basics for a week.
  • ☐ I wash with lukewarm water and pat dry.
