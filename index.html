05 / Questions

Straight answers.

Will it shrink?

A little cotton always moves, but ours is washed before it's cut. Expect it to stay within a few millimeters.

Is it worth the price?

One tee that lasts years costs less than three that warp by summer.

What if it doesn't fit?

Swap it for a different size within 30 days. Free.

How should I wash it?

Cold, inside out, hang dry if you can. It'll forgive you if you don't.
