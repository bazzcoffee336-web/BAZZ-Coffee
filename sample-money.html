The problem

Payday feels good. A week later you check your balance and think: "Where did it all go?" You can't remember spending that much, but the number doesn't lie.

Why it happens

Small payments are invisible. Card taps, subscriptions, delivery fees and "just this once" purchases don't feel like spending because no cash leaves your hand. Our brains remember big purchases and forget the small, frequent ones. But the small ones add up the fastest.

The fix: the 30-day money snapshot

You don't need a budget yet. First, you need the truth.

  1. Download the last 30 days of transactions from your bank and card apps.
  2. Sort every line into 5 groups: Home (rent, utilities), Food (groceries and eating out separately), Getting around (fuel, transport), Debt payments, Everything else.
  3. Circle 3 surprises. The categories that are bigger than you expected.
  4. List every subscription. Streaming, apps, memberships, free trials that turned into payments.

Most people find at least one subscription they forgot, and one category that's much bigger than they thought.

Try it now

Open your bank app, scroll back 30 days, and write down every subscription you see. Cancel one you don't use today.

Check yourself

  • ☐ I know my total spending for the last 30 days.
  • ☐ I know my 3 biggest surprise categories.
  • ☐ I cancelled at least one unused subscription.
