Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What you buy

All BAZZ products are digital ebooks in PDF format. There is nothing shipped. You can download your ebook right after your payment is confirmed.

No refunds after download

Because a PDF can't be returned once you have it, all sales are final. Before you pay, we ask you to tick a box agreeing that we start your download straight away and that you lose your right to cancel once the download is available to you.

Customers in the EU and UK

Normally you may cancel an online purchase within 14 days. For digital content, this right ends once delivery has started with your express consent and your acknowledgement that you lose the right to cancel. That is exactly what the checkbox at checkout asks for. If you do not tick it, you cannot complete the purchase.

If something goes wrong

Your rights for a faulty product always stay in place. If your file won't download, won't open, is damaged, or isn't the book you ordered, email hello@bazzbooks.store with your order details. We will send you a working copy, and if we can't fix the problem, we will refund you.

Charged twice?

If you were charged more than once for the same order, email us and we will refund the extra payment.
