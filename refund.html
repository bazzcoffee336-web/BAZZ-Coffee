Refund & Withdrawal Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version

  • 📘 Every BAZZ book is a digital PDF. You can download it right after you pay.
  • ✅ Before you pay, you tick a box agreeing that your download starts straight away.
  • 🔒 Once your download is available, the sale is final and your right of withdrawal ends.
  • 🛠️ If your file is broken or wrong, we fix it. Always.

1. What you buy

BAZZ sells digital ebooks in PDF format. Nothing is shipped. After your payment is confirmed, you can download your ebook straight away and read it on any phone, tablet or computer.

2. Your right of withdrawal

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you normally have the right to withdraw from an online purchase without giving a reason.

For digital content this right ends early. It ends as soon as we start providing your download, as long as you have, before paying:

  1. expressly agreed that we start the download before the withdrawal period ends, and
  2. confirmed that you know you lose your right of withdrawal by doing so.

That is exactly what the checkbox before checkout asks you to confirm. You cannot pay without ticking it. After payment, we send you an email confirming your agreement.

3. All sales are final

Because a PDF can't be returned once you have it, we do not offer refunds if you change your mind, didn't read the book, or bought the wrong title by mistake after the download became available. Please read each book's page carefully before you buy. You can see real pages from every book under “Look inside”.

4. When we always help

Your legal rights for a faulty product stay in place. Email us and we will sort it out if:

  • your file won't download or won't open,
  • the file is damaged or incomplete,
  • you received a different book than the one you paid for,
  • you were charged twice for the same order.

5. How to contact us

Email info@bazzbooks.store with:

  • the email address you used to buy,
  • the book title and the date of your order,
  • what went wrong (a screenshot helps).

We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method.

6. Withdrawal before your download

If, for any reason, your download has not been made available to you yet, you can still withdraw. Just tell us clearly by email, for example:

“I hereby withdraw from my purchase of [book title], ordered on [date]. Name: … Email used: …”
