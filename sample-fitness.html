Chapter 1: "I don't have time."

The problem

Between work, family and everything else, an hour at the gym feels impossible. So you do nothing, and wait for a week with more time. That week never comes.

Why it happens

We think exercise only counts if it's long and hard. So short sessions feel pointless, and long sessions don't fit. The result: zero. But official guidelines say activity adds up across the week, and short sessions count.

The fix: the 20-minute rule

  1. 20 minutes is your full workout. Not a warm-up for something bigger. The whole thing.
  2. Find your slot. Before work, at lunch, after dinner. Pick the same time each day if you can.
  3. Do the math once. 2 strength sessions (Chapter 3) + walking (Chapter 8) on other days can get you to the 150-minute guideline.
  4. On busy days, do 10. Two 10-minute blocks are far better than nothing.
  5. Schedule it in your calendar like a meeting.

Try it now

Open your calendar and block 20 minutes on 3 days this week. Name it "Workout".

Check yourself

  • ☐ I have 3 workout slots in my calendar.
  • ☐ I know my backup plan is 10 minutes.
  • ☐ I understand short sessions count.
